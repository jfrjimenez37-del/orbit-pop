## English (en-US) — default

**Short description (max 80):**
One tap to flip your orbit. Dodge, duel friends and conquer 16 wild worlds!

**Full description:**
Spin around the planet and don't let anything touch you!

ORBIT POP is a one-tap arcade game: your little critter races around in orbit and every tap flips its direction. Dodge meteors, spiky mines, laser beams and shockwaves, grab coins and see how far you can go. Easy to learn, hard to master.

🌍 16 WILD WORLDS
Sky, lagoon, sunset, candy, night... then twin, three and four-planet systems where you jump from orbit to orbit, and planets that dance, swing and pulse while you play. Climb from the clouds to deep space!

☄️ A NEW OBSTACLE IN EVERY WORLD
Boomerangs that come back, satellites, solar flares, UFOs dropping mines, asteroid belts, meteors that split in two and pulsar beams. Every one you meet joins your obstacle collection.

⚡ LEVEL UP AND GET STRONGER
Earn XP every run and unlock 8 permanent powers: shield, coin magnet, slow-mo (hold your finger down), teleport (swipe), bubble boost, head start, second life and bonus coins. Upgrade them with the coins you collect.

⚔️ DUEL YOUR FRIENDS
Send a challenge: your friend plays the exact same run, no powers, one try. Highest score wins!

📅 DAILY CHALLENGE AND WEEKLY EVENTS
A new run every day, the same for everyone. And every week a special event (comet storm, mine field, laser night, speed week...) with coin rewards and its own leaderboard.

🎨 29 SKINS AND 22 TRAILS
Common, rare, epic, legendary and mythic characters, some with special effects. Unlock them by beating your record, leveling up or spending coins; the rarest ones shine with a special aura.

🏆 COMPETE
Global and regional leaderboards with Google Play Games, 34 achievements, 3 daily missions and a weekly challenge.

✨ FAIR BY DESIGN
Every obstacle pattern is checked before it appears, with time to react: there is always a way out.

• Quick runs, perfect for any moment
• Original pixel art and chiptune music
• Plays offline
• Free to play, with ads and optional purchases
